# Visual Direction — What If Humans Never Needed Sleep?

## System
- Format: 16:9, 1920x1080 delivery; authored source plates are 1600x900 SVG.
- Palette: Deep navy `#0b0d12`, warm off-white `#eae7e1`, amber `#ffb340`.
- Style: editorial scientific line art, bold geometric silhouettes, large negative space, no stock imagery, no photorealistic faces, no decorative text baked into assets.
- Motion: Remotion applies slow push/pan and alternates layout every beat; authored plate changes at scene boundaries while typography/callouts change every 3–6 seconds.
- Safety: diagrams are conceptual, not medical illustrations; narration carries factual qualification.

## Scene plates
1. **HOOK** — bed silhouette contrasted with an oversized clock; visually communicates the last night of sleep and reclaimed waking time.
2. **YOU GAIN A SECOND LIFE** — hourglass plus rising activity skyline/trajectory; communicates recovered annual hours without implying literal lifespan extension.
3. **SLEEP IS DOING WORK** — abstract brain network plus checklist; communicates memory, attention, immune/repair functions without anatomical overclaiming.
4. **NIGHT STILL EXISTS INSIDE YOU** — sun/moon duality over a continuous waveform; communicates circadian timing surviving the removal of sleep need.
5. **THE 24-HOUR ECONOMY** — illuminated city blocks and clock; communicates institutions operating continuously and economic pressure expanding into unused hours.
6. **SLEEP WAS A BOUNDARY** — phone/message field separated by a strong vertical boundary and clock; communicates protected unavailability rather than biological rest.
7. **HOMES AND RELATIONSHIPS CHANGE** — two adjacent homes/people divided by opposite clocks; communicates schedule desynchronization and deliberate overlap.
8. **WHAT HAPPENS TO MEMORY AND TIME?** — continuous timeline passing through multiple clocks; communicates days losing natural chapter breaks.
9. **PAYOFF** — central clock with human silhouettes and a protective arc; communicates the final question: who owns the recovered time?

## Caption / SFX intent
- Captions remain clean lower-third subtitles with no word-by-word karaoke effect.
- Use restrained soft ticks only for major time transitions, a low tonal rise at the hook paradox, and a subtle stop/mute cue at the boundary scene. No constant background SFX bed.
- Do not add sound effects until an independent QA pass confirms they improve comprehension rather than clutter.
